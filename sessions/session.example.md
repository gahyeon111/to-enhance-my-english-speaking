# 2026-01-01 — 자주 가는 공원

## 질문

1. Tell me about a park you often visit.
2. Tell me about a memorable experience you had there.
3. Your friend cannot find the park. Call your friend and give directions.

## 사용자 원문(STT)

> There is a park near my home. I usually go there on weekends. Last weekend, I go there with a friend and we walked around the lake.

## 핵심 평가

- 장소와 평소 습관을 구체적으로 설명했다.
- 과거 경험에서는 동사 시제를 일관되게 유지할 필요가 있다.

## 주요 교정

| 원문 | 추천 표현 | 이유 |
|---|---|---|
| Last weekend, I go there. | Last weekend, I went there. | 과거 경험이므로 과거형 사용 |

## 개선된 답변

### Q1

There is a park near my home. I usually go there on weekends because it is quiet and relaxing.

### Q2

Last weekend, I went there with a friend, and we walked around the lake.

## 새로 수집한 표현

- 호수를 한 바퀴 걷다: `walk around the lake`
